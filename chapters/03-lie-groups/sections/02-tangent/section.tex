\section{The tangent space and the Lie algebra}\label{sec:lie:tangent}

Where can a group element move? At each point of the manifold the possible instantaneous velocities form a flat vector space, the tangent space. The one at the identity is special: the group structure lets it describe velocities everywhere, and it carries the name \emph{Lie algebra}. This section derives it for every group with one recipe: differentiate the constraint.
