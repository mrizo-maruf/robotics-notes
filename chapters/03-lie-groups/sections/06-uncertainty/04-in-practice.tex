\subsection{In practice: covariances in messages and solvers}\label{sec:lie:uncertainty-in-practice}

\texttt{geometry\_msgs/PoseWithCovariance} carries a $6\times6$ covariance. Its definition says the order is $(x,y,z,$ rotation about $X$, about $Y$, about $Z)$, and that ``the orientation parameters use a fixed-axis representation''. It does \emph{not} say whether the perturbation is applied in the body frame or the world frame, i.e.\ left or right in this chapter's terms. When feeding a ROS covariance into a Lie-group filter, find out what the publisher meant and, if needed, convert with $\Ad$ (\S\ref{sec:lie:covariance}). Note also the ordering: translation first, then rotation, like this book. Barfoot and Furgale (\emph{Associating Uncertainty with Three-Dimensional Poses for Use in Estimation Problems}, IEEE T-RO 2014) give the standard treatment of $\SE$ uncertainty, including compounding and fusion of uncertain poses.
