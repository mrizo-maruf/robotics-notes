\section{Exp, Log, plus and minus}\label{sec:lie:exp-plus}

Chapters~\ref{ch:pl} and~\ref{ch:rot} derived an exponential and a logarithm separately for each object, each time from a different picture (Rodrigues' decomposition, the turn centre, the half-angle sandwich). Here they are derived once, for every Lie group, from one physical statement: move with constant local velocity for unit time. Two operators built on them, $\oplus$ and $\ominus$, then let us write all of calculus and estimation as if the group were a vector space.
