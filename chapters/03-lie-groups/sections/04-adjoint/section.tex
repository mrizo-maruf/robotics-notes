\section{The adjoint: moving increments between frames}\label{sec:lie:adjoint}

A tangent vector is always ``a velocity or increment \emph{expressed in some frame}''. The adjoint is the linear map that re-expresses it in another frame. It has appeared three times already without the name: as $\omega_s=R\omega_b$ (\S\ref{sec:rot:angular-velocity-kinematics}), as the twist transformation of \S\ref{sec:rot:changing-frame-twist}, and as the bridge between left and right plus above.
