\section{Lie theory at work in robotics}\label{sec:lie:robotics}

With $\oplus$, $\ominus$, the adjoint, the elementary Jacobians and tangent-space covariances, the two workhorses of robot state estimation can be written down for any Lie group with no special cases: the error-state Kalman filter and the pose-graph (nonlinear least-squares) optimiser. This section builds both on $\SEtwo$, following Solà et al.'s Section V, and then surveys where else the same structure carries robotics.
